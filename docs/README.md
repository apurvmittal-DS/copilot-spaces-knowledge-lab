# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

## Process overview

OctoAcme projects begin with initiation, where teams validate the business need, align stakeholders, define measurable success, identify initial risks, and confirm resources. Approved initiatives move into planning, where the team creates a prioritized backlog, estimates work, establishes a release timeline, documents dependencies and risks, and agrees on a Definition of Done.

Delivery uses a shared project board and a predictable team rhythm, including daily standups, weekly delivery and PM–Product Manager syncs, demos, and stakeholder updates. The Project Manager coordinates plans, schedules, risks, and communication; the Product Manager defines outcomes and priorities; Developers implement and test the solution; and QA/Testing validates quality and acceptance. Risks and blockers are monitored in a risk register and escalated through team, PM, Product Lead, and Sponsor levels as needed.

Quality is integrated into execution through small pull requests, issue links, acceptance criteria, automated tests and linting, security scanning, peer review, and test coverage appropriate to each change. Before release, teams confirm acceptance criteria, passing CI checks, smoke tests, release notes, rollback or mitigation plans, and stakeholder readiness. Deployments are staged where appropriate and followed by verification and communication.

After each sprint, release, incident, or important milestone, retrospectives capture what worked and what can improve. Findings become prioritized, owned action items with due dates and success criteria, which are tracked in the backlog and reviewed regularly. This approach supports continuous improvement while keeping delivery decisions, responsibilities, and outcomes visible to the team.
